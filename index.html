How a number gets on the board.

No silent fallbacks. A value carries its on-chain proof or an explicit reason it could not be computed.

1

Registry

Every B20 token from the Coinbase tokenized-stocks API, hash-pinned. Supply above zero means minted.

2

Verify on-chain

Each pool with at least $5k liquidity is read by eth_call at a pinned block. V4 reads go through the PoolManager.

3

Compare to NAV

Official Chainlink NAV feeds give the anchor. Tokens without one are labeled, not guessed.

4

Simulate depth

Sell ladders of $1k, $5k and $25k run through real on-chain quoters. A simulation, never an execution quote.

Snapshot coverage and RPC checks

375 pool records scanned; 82 verified and priced on-chain. Skipped records are not counted as priced. Token blocks differ by collection slice; this snapshot has not been refreshed.

Two primary public Base RPC endpoints (mainnet.base.org and publicnode) are compared byte-for-byte. A third endpoint (BlastAPI) replaces one primary only after a transport failure; it is not a third vote. Two matching responses are required; a mismatch or only one usable response hides the value.
